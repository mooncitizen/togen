---
title: Exporting diagrams
description: Rendering the open view to an image.
---

Export, in the top bar, renders the open view to a PNG or JPEG in the browser, from the same drawing the canvas shows: boundary, labels and icons included.

You can choose:

- the scale, 1x, 2x or 3x
- the ground: the theme's own, white (which takes the light palette), or for PNG, nothing at all
- whether the view's name appears in the corner

![The export dialog, with scale, ground and label options and a preview of the rendered view](/togen/images/export-dialog.png)

The dialog previews the picture and states its size in pixels. Saving downloads the file through the browser as `<project>-<view>.png` or `.jpg`.
