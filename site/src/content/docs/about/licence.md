title: Licence
description: Togen is Apache-2.0, free for any use, and what attribution actually means for it.
---

Togen is licensed under Apache License 2.0. Free for any use, including commercial, including inside a product you sell. The full text is [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/mooncitizen/togen/blob/main/LICENSE) in the repository.

Apache-2.0 asks for attribution when you redistribute Togen itself, its source or the binary, under the licence's own notice terms. What it cannot do, no permissive open source licence can, is require credit from someone who merely runs the tool and ships what it produced. Generated Terraform is your output, not a distribution of Togen, and nothing in the licence follows it out the door.

That is deliberate rather than a gap. The credit Togen actually asks for is the line it writes itself, first in every file it generates:

```hcl
# Generated by Togen - https://github.com/mooncitizen/togen
```

Leave it in place and that is the whole of what the project asks from a generated repository. [`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/mooncitizen/togen/blob/main/CITATION.cff) says the same thing for anyone citing the project directly.

[`NOTICE`](https://github.com/mooncitizen/togen/blob/main/NOTICE) in the repository covers copyright and one more thing worth knowing about: the studio bundles each provider's own architecture icon set, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, and each stays under its vendor's own terms, not Togen's licence. The full notice for each sits beside the files it covers, and the studio's About panel shows all three.
